Orbit Cube Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Cube handles information when you use the Orbit Cube mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Orbit Cube itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Orbit Cube uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may automatically collect and process certain information through the advertising SDK, including device identifiers, IP address, information about interactions with the app, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected through Google Mobile Ads may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising services, as well as to detect fraud and maintain the security and performance of the advertising service.

3. Advertising

Orbit Cube uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use information collected through the Mobile Ads SDK in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

For more information about how Google uses information when you use apps or websites that use its services, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Orbit Cube uses Google's consent management technology to request user consent for certain types of data processing and personalized advertising.

Users may be presented with privacy and advertising choices and may change or withdraw consent where applicable.
